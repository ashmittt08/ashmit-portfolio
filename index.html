MockMate: AI-Powered Mock Interview Platform

The Brief: An interview preparation platform that helps users improve their technical and behavioral interview skills — generating interview questions, evaluating responses, and providing actionable feedback.

The System: Google Gemini generates role-specific questions and analyzes answers. A Node.js + Express API handles sessions, Firebase manages authentication, and Prisma persists interview history to PostgreSQL.

02 // Narrative Bio

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at VIT Bhopal University, specializing in Gaming Technology. My interests sit at the intersection of software engineering, artificial intelligence, and interactive technology.

I enjoy building full-stack applications with React, TypeScript, Node.js, Express, and PostgreSQL, while also experimenting with generative AI and LLM-powered products. My background in gaming technology has also led me to work with Unity, C#, Vuforia, and 3D/AR development.

03 // Engineering Tenets

I. Understand the Problem

[ 01 ]

Before writing code, figure out who the product is for and what actually needs solving. Everything after depends on getting this right.

II. Design the System

[ 02 ]

Data models, API boundaries, authentication, and the flow between client and server — planned so the pieces fit before they are built.

III. Build, Then Improve

[ 03 ]

Ship a working version, learn from it, and keep refining. A deployed project teaches more than a perfect plan.

"Understand the problem, design the system, build the product — and continuously improve it."
